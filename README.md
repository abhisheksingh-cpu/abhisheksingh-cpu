## `whoami`

I'm a Computer Science student specializing in Artificial Intelligence, interested in building systems that solve real engineering problems.

I enjoy exploring what happens behind the abstractions — from how APIs communicate and databases preserve state to how AI systems reason over information and how distributed systems recover from failure.

I believe the best way to learn engineering is to **build things, understand why they work, and investigate why they break.**

- Exploring **AI Engineering, Backend Development & Distributed Systems**
- Building practical projects with real-world engineering constraints
- Learning system design, infrastructure, and production-oriented development
- Turning ideas into working software, one commit at a time
